Find it

Box tracker or text prompt

Draw a box and a centre-weighted correlation tracker, helped by motion cues, follows it. Or type what it is: DETR finds it and SAM cuts an exact mask.

Follow the camera

Motion estimation

The camera's movement is estimated frame to frame, ignoring the object being erased, so pans don't smear the background.

Fill the gap

Real pixels first

Hidden background is copied from nearby frames where it is visible. Anything never seen is reconstructed from its surroundings.

Measured

Tested against ground truth

On a synthetic clip with a known clean background, quality in the erased area goes from 12.7 dB to 36.3 dB PSNR. Tests run in CI.
